Roadmap

Where Staple goes next, and what's already here.

No dates. Staple is built by one person, and a date here would be a promise rather than a plan. What's ordered is the sequence, and you decide it — the poll in the beta Discord is what moves something up.

Making it feel finished.

  • A proper design pass on the rating screen

  • Better headers across the app

Ideas, not commitments

Interesting, unscheduled, and some of it may never happen. Most of this is where a paid tier would live if there ever is one — rating, ranking and everything social stay free, always.

  • Personal taste analytics — your price-band bias, your cuisines, how you drift
  • Trip and city planning mode
  • Scan a dish to tag it, running on your phone rather than a server
  • Advanced filters, and an extra decimal place
  • Export, and full offline access
  • Unlimited lists and tags, and a profile you can make your own

Not building, on purpose

Leaderboards, streaks, and anything that turns eating out into a competition. Those are what break every other app in this category, and they're refused rather than deferred.

No public feed either — meaning no stream of strangers' meals to scroll, no directory of people to browse, nothing you arrive at uninvited. A community average shown beside a restaurant is not that: it's one number for context, and it's there precisely because it keeps scores honest. Whose opinions you actually see are the people you chose to follow.
