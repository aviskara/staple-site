Roadmap

What's built, what's coming, and what's still an idea.

Everything below is grouped by what it's for, not by when it lands. There are no dates — Staple is built by one person, and a date on this page would be a promise rather than a plan. The order comes from you: the poll in the beta Discord decides what gets built next.

in your build now not yet

Photos

  • Taken at the table and kept on your phone
  • Backed up, so a new phone keeps them
  • One per dish, not one per meal

Friends, where you actually decide

  • Follow someone and see what they want to go to
  • Their scores on the place page itself, while you're deciding
  • A notification when someone follows you

Deciding together

  • Propose places, rank them, and vote — inside the app
  • A link anyone can vote on, with no app and no account
    • The people you're eating with must never need to install anything

A ranking that's actually ranked

  • Break ties within a price band, instead of by what you ate most recently
  • Move a place up or down by hand when the order is wrong

Polish, and looking back

  • A proper design pass on the rating screen
  • Your year in value finds — built on how honestly you rate, not how much

Not building, on purpose

Leaderboards, streaks, a public feed of strangers, and anything that makes eating out feel like a competition. Those are what break every other app in this category, and they're refused rather than deferred.
